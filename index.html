How CGPA is calculated:
CGPA = (Previous CGPA × Previous Credits + Semester Grade Points) ÷ (Previous Credits + Semester Credits)

About This Tool

A lightweight academic utility built for students of the University of Engineering & Technology. It helps you quickly calculate your semester GPA and running CGPA using the official UET 4.00 grading scale.

Features

  • Supports up to 20 subjects per semester
  • Official UET A–F grade point scale
  • Cumulative CGPA with prior semester data
  • Works fully offline in your browser
