Official UET 4.00 Grading Policy & Summer Regulations

Letter grades, grade points, repeating rules, and summer credit limits

Summer Semester Policy

  • Students can register for a maximum of 8 Credit Hours during a Summer Semester.
  • Summer courses are primarily used for Grade Improvement (Repeat) or clearing failed (F) subjects.
  • Withdrawing from a summer subject by the 3rd week results in a "W" grade (no GPA impact).

Grade Improvement & Replacement (R) Rules

  • When a student retakes/improves a course, the new grade replaces the old grade in CGPA calculation!
  • The old grade points are removed and replaced by the new grade points, preventing double counting of credit hours.
  • Improved subjects are suffixed with "R" on the official UET transcript.
